# Visual Critique
## When to use
Use after a visual artifact exists and needs evidence-based quality review before approval or repair.
## Required inputs
Artifact reference, normalized brief, fixed constraints, and evaluation criteria.
## Step sequence
1. Check hard constraints first. 2. Assess hierarchy and readability. 3. Inspect visual defects and consistency. 4. Separate must-fix defects from optional alternatives. 5. Prioritize repairs.
## Design heuristics
Critique against intent and constraints, not personal taste; make every recommendation actionable.
## Constraints
Do not silently redesign the brief or create infinite self-repair loops.
## Verification checklist
Confirm each issue cites a criterion, severity, affected region, and suggested bounded action.
## Failure modes
Taste-based scoring, vague criticism, inconsistent severity, and moving goalposts.
## Examples
Synthetic example: flag a fictional poster’s clipped legal copy as a hard defect while treating alternate image crop as an optional improvement.
## What not to do
Do not reject a valid design merely because a different style is also possible.
