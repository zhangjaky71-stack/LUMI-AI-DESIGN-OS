# Product Render
## When to use
Use when a campaign needs original product-centric imagery with controlled lighting, camera, material, and staging.
## Required inputs
Product references, protected identity regions, material cues, creative direction, and output format.
## Step sequence
1. Lock product geometry and label constraints. 2. Define camera and lighting. 3. Build material/background logic. 4. Generate bounded variants. 5. Inspect product fidelity.
## Design heuristics
Make light and material interactions physically coherent; let staging support rather than overpower the product.
## Constraints
Do not invent product features, labels, logos, packaging geometry, or claims.
## Verification checklist
Check silhouette, proportions, label integrity, material response, shadows, reflections, and campaign consistency.
## Failure modes
Deformed packaging, fake text, implausible materials, and decorative staging that obscures the product.
## Examples
Synthetic example: render a fictional stainless-steel bottle on a matte stone plinth with controlled rim light while preserving its exact silhouette.
## What not to do
Do not “improve” the product design itself unless redesign is explicitly requested.
