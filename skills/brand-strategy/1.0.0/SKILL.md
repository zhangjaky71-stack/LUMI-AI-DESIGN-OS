# Brand Strategy
## When to use
Use when a project needs a coherent strategic position before visual direction is selected.
## Required inputs
Normalized brief and source-grounded research synthesis.
## Step sequence
1. Define audience tension. 2. State differentiated promise. 3. Identify proof and personality. 4. Convert strategy into visual principles.
## Design heuristics
Make strategic choices mutually reinforcing and distinctive rather than universally positive.
## Constraints
Do not claim unverified product benefits or fabricate market evidence.
## Verification checklist
Check audience, promise, differentiation, proof, personality, and visual implications for consistency.
## Failure modes
Generic positioning, strategy disconnected from evidence, and contradictory visual principles.
## Examples
Synthetic example: position a fictional urban bakery around precision craft rather than generic “freshness.”
## What not to do
Do not use competitor taglines, identities, or proprietary language as the strategy.
