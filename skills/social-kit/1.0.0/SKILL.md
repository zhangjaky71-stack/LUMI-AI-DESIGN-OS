# Social Kit
## When to use
Use after a key visual is approved and needs systematic adaptation across social formats.
## Required inputs
Approved key visual, content variants, platform dimensions, and protected layout rules.
## Step sequence
1. Identify invariant brand elements. 2. Map hierarchy to each ratio. 3. Re-crop rather than stretch imagery. 4. Validate copy and safe areas per format.
## Design heuristics
Preserve hierarchy and concept while allowing composition to reflow for each aspect ratio.
## Constraints
Do not shrink critical text below readable thresholds or crop protected product/identity regions.
## Verification checklist
Check all dimensions, hierarchy consistency, crop quality, copy accuracy, and brand invariants.
## Failure modes
Mechanical resizing, inconsistent type scale, unsafe crops, and format-specific overflow.
## Examples
Synthetic example: adapt a fictional 4:5 campaign to 1:1 and 9:16 by changing image crop and line breaks while keeping the same visual thesis.
## What not to do
Do not force a desktop or poster composition into every social ratio unchanged.
