# Poster Design
## When to use
Use when producing a poster or campaign key visual from an already approved design system.
## Required inputs
Creative direction, typography, layout rules, copy, asset references, and output dimensions.
## Step sequence
1. Preserve mandatory content. 2. Establish focal hierarchy. 3. Integrate assets without distortion. 4. Apply spacing and type rules. 5. Validate at final dimensions.
## Design heuristics
A poster should communicate one dominant message quickly and reward closer reading with secondary detail.
## Constraints
Never move fixed elements or alter protected assets unless the brief explicitly permits it.
## Verification checklist
Check exact size, copy fidelity, safe areas, hierarchy, asset integrity, and export readiness.
## Failure modes
Template imitation, over-decoration, copy errors, and violating fixed QR/logo/navigation zones.
## Examples
Synthetic example: a fictional food launch poster uses one oversized product image, compact price block, and restrained supporting copy.
## What not to do
Do not add buttons, logos, claims, or decorative text that the brief did not authorize.
