# Brief Normalization
## When to use
Use before planning when the request has incomplete or conflicting design requirements.
## Required inputs
User brief, project constraints, known assets, and desired deliverables.
## Step sequence
1. Extract goals and audience. 2. Separate hard constraints from preferences. 3. Record unknowns without inventing facts. 4. Produce acceptance criteria.
## Design heuristics
Prefer explicit measurable constraints and preserve user intent over speculative detail.
## Constraints
Do not manufacture brand facts, rights, dimensions, deadlines, or approvals.
## Verification checklist
Confirm goals, deliverables, fixed constraints, open assumptions, and acceptance criteria are represented.
## Failure modes
Hidden assumptions, omitted fixed constraints, or turning preferences into requirements.
## Examples
Synthetic example: convert “make a premium launch poster” into audience, format, tone, must-keep copy, asset references, and review criteria.
## What not to do
Do not begin generation before the brief is coherent enough to validate.
