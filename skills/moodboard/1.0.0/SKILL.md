# Moodboard
## When to use
Use to make an approved creative direction visually concrete before production design begins.
## Required inputs
Creative direction, asset references, and research references with provenance.
## Step sequence
1. Select references by principle. 2. Balance composition, type, material, image, and color cues. 3. Label why each reference matters. 4. Remove redundant or dominant sources.
## Design heuristics
Use multiple independent references so the board communicates principles rather than a template to imitate.
## Constraints
Do not imply ownership of external references and do not reproduce them as final deliverables.
## Verification checklist
Confirm provenance, principle coverage, visual diversity, and absence of one-source dominance.
## Failure modes
Reference dumping, stylistic contradiction, and copying a single campaign structure.
## Examples
Synthetic example: a board for a fictional tea brand uses separate references for paper texture, editorial spacing, botanical scale, and muted lighting.
## What not to do
Do not make the final design a collage of external copyrighted assets.
